Privacy Policy for Naaply

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Naaply ("we", "our", or "the app") is an offline tailoring order and measurement management application created by Orion PK, designed for tailors and fashion makers.

This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled, why device permissions are requested, and how your privacy is protected.

1. Offline-First Local Data Storage

Naaply works primarily offline. All customer records, measurement profiles (naap), order statuses, delivery dates, and payment records are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or upload your customer or measurement data to our servers.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

The app requests the following device permissions solely to provide its features:

  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS): Used solely to temporarily add customer phone numbers to your device's address book so you can directly send order slips and receipts via WhatsApp without typing the number manually. Your contacts are never uploaded or shared.
  • Notifications and Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used solely to set and display on-device alerts reminding you of upcoming order delivery deadlines.
  • Photos and Storage: Used when you choose to export, save, or share generated order receipt images to your device gallery or messaging apps.
  • Internet and Network: Used for optional usage and crash reports (see section 4), checking for app updates, and optional user-initiated Google Drive backups.

3. Google Sign-In and Google Drive Backup (Optional)

Cloud backup is entirely optional and initiated by the user. If you choose to use cloud backup:

  • You sign in with your own Google Account.
  • Your database backup is uploaded directly to your own Google Drive storage (in your account's private app data folder).
  • We cannot read, view, or access your Google Drive files.
  • You can delete your backups from your Google Drive at any time.

4. Usage Reports and Crash Reports (Optional)

The first time you open Naaply you are asked whether to send usage reports. Nothing is sent until you answer, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings under "استعمال کی رپورٹس بھیجیں".

If you say yes, the app sends the following to Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC):

  • Usage events: that an action happened — for example an order was created, a receipt was shared, a backup completed, or a setting was changed — together with non-identifying details such as which garment type, whether a due date was set, the order amount as a number, and how many measurement fields were filled.
  • Screens: which screens of the app are opened and for how long.
  • About your shop, as ranges only: how many customers, measurement profiles and orders it has (for example "6–20"), the unit setting, whether you are signed in to Google and when you last backed up, and whether notification and contacts permissions were granted.
  • About the app and device: app version, device model, Android version, language and country, and a randomly generated identifier for your shop. This identifier is created by the app, is not derived from you or your device, and is not linked to your name, phone number or Google account.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes or an operation such as sign-in or backup fails, a technical report (the error, a stack trace, device model, Android version and the same random identifier) so we can fix it.

What is never sent: customer names, phone numbers, cities or addresses, measurements, notes, receipt contents, anything you type into a search box, your shop's name, or your Google e-mail address.

  • Reports are used only to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. They are not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than Google as the processor of this service.
  • Google retains event data for 14 months.
  • No Advertising ID (AD_ID): We do not collect Google Advertising IDs and we do not serve advertisements or track you across other apps.
  • WhatsApp: Used via external app intents to let you share order slips with customers upon your explicit request.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored on your device:

  • Deleting a customer or order within the app removes it from the active database.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage deletes all locally stored data from your device.
  • You can create or delete manual ZIP backups of your data at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

Naaply is a commercial tool for tailoring businesses and professionals. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
